# MY-CHURCH-DAIRY
In my journal I wrote and I will about how I found my heavenly father and what things are very important to me in my life. I have some testimonies as confirmation that miracles exist and we are not alone and that the holy spirit exists as our heavenly father. I believe that The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is the only true church.<br>
