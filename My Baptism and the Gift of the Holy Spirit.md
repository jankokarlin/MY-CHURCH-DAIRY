# My Baptism 13.07.2024 and the Gift of the Holy Spirit 14.07.2024
On 13.7.2024 at 10 am I was baptized. Elder Tanner baptized me. It was an incredible feeling.<br>
On 14.7.2024 I received the gift of the Holy Ghost. Even more incredible feelings, I always have a smile on my face when I remember the baptism and the gift of the Holy Ghost.<br>
Sisters Stutzman and Paulsen helped me. At the baptism I had another sister, Lines, who came from Serbia. Sister Stutzman went to Maribor to serve. However, my sister Stutzman is leaving on November 1st back to America. Thank you for everything.<br>
<br>
13.7.2024 ob 10 uri sem imel krst. Krstil me je starešina Tanner. Bili so neverjetni občutki.<br>
14.7.2024 sem prejel dar svetega duha. Še bolj neverjetni občutki, Vedno , ko sem spomnin na krst in dar svetega duha imam nasmešek na obrazu.<br>
Pomagali sta mi sestri Stutzman in Paulsen. Na krstu sem imel že drugo sestro Lines, ki je prišla iz srbije. Sestra Stutzman je odšla v Maribor na služenje. Vendar moja sestra Stutzman odhaja s 1.Novembrom nazaj v ameriko. Hvala ji za vse.<br>
