# 7. Visiting the Temple in Frankfurt
In August 2025, I was in the Temple - the house of the Lord - for the second time. The feelings were great because I was baptized for my parents, relatives and learned what it is like in the temple itself when you do something good for your deceased relatives.<br>
<br>
V avgustu 2025 sem bil že drugič v Templju - Gospodovi hiši. Občutki so bili odlični, ker sem se  krstil za starše, sorodnike in spoznal kako je v samem templju, ko narediš nekaj dobrega za umrle svojce.
